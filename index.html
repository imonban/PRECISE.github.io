FUSE-PDAC Multimodal AI Fusion Model for Early Detection
of Pancreatic Cancer

Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) is diagnosed late in over 85% of cases, when curative resection is no longer an option. FUSE-PDAC fuses pre-cancerous CT imaging biomarkers with electronic medical record data through a graph neural network — the PRECISE model — to enable opportunistic, equitable risk stratification at population scale.

The PRECISE Model

Fusing Imaging & Clinical Data
to Predict PDAC Risk

The core algorithmic contribution: a multimodal graph neural network that jointly encodes abdominopelvic CT biomarkers and longitudinal EMR data to produce unbiased, patient-specific risk scores.

Specific Aims

Three Aims, One Goal:
Catch PDAC Before It's Too Late

Each aim builds toward a deployable, fair, prospectively validated risk prediction tool embedded in routine clinical CT workflows.

01

Aim 1 · Biomarker Discovery

Extract and Validate Pre-Cancerous CT Imaging Biomarkers

Develop and validate a deep learning pipeline to extract quantitative imaging biomarkers — parenchymal texture, ductal morphology, peripancreatic fat infiltration, and vascular geometry — from abdominopelvic CTs acquired 6–36 months before PDAC diagnosis. Biomarkers will be validated against histopathological ground truth across multi-institutional datasets.

CT · Radiomics · Deep Learning
02

Aim 2 · Multimodal Fusion Model

Develop PRECISE: A Fair, Graph-Based Multimodal Risk Model

Train the PRECISE graph neural network to fuse imaging biomarkers (Aim 1) with longitudinal EMR features — CA19-9 trajectories, diabetes onset patterns, weight loss signals, and prior pancreatitis history. Incorporate adversarial debiasing to ensure equitable performance across race, sex, and socioeconomic strata. Benchmark against clinical risk scores (ENDPAC, CAPS) and unimodal AI baselines.

GNN · EMR · Fairness · Graph Fusion
03

Aim 3 · Prospective Deployment

Prospective Multi-Site Validation and Deployment

Deploy PRECISE prospectively across geographically and demographically diverse clinical sites, including Mayo Clinic (Arizona, Minnesota), partner academic medical centers, and a community health system. Assess real-world calibration, net benefit in decision curve analysis, and disparities in model performance by site, race/ethnicity, and sex over a 24-month follow-up period.

Prospective · Multi-Site · Deployment

Technical Pipeline

From Routine CT to Risk Score

The PRECISE pipeline is designed for opportunistic deployment — patients undergoing routine abdominopelvic CT for non-pancreatic indications are automatically screened.

🩻

CT Acquisition

Routine abdomino-pelvic CT, portal venous phase

🧠

Image Segmentation

Auto-segmentation of pancreas, ducts, vasculature

📋

EMR Extraction

Longitudinal labs, diagnoses, medications, NLP

🔗

PRECISE GNN

Graph fusion with adversarial debiasing

📊

Risk Score

Calibrated 3-yr PDAC risk, EHR-integrated report

⚖️

Equity by Design

Adversarial debiasing is embedded in training, not post-hoc. The model is penalized during training if subgroup AUC diverges beyond a pre-specified fairness threshold across race, sex, and BMI strata.

🔬

Explainable Outputs

SHAP-based feature attribution surfaces the top contributing imaging and clinical factors for each prediction, enabling radiologist review and physician trust calibration.

🔌

EHR Integration

PRECISE interfaces with Epic via FHIR R4 APIs. Risk scores and contributing features are returned as a structured CDS Hooks card within the ordering clinician's workflow.

Project Timeline

Milestones & Progress

Project period: September 2024 – August 2029. Year 1 focus is dataset assembly, segmentation pipeline development, and IRB coordination across sites.

Year 1 · Sep 2024 – Aug 2025

Project Launch & Data Infrastructure

IRB approvals at all sites; retrospective PDAC cohort assembly (≥1,200 cases + 4:1 controls); CT segmentation pipeline development; baseline imaging biomarker extraction; EMR data harmonization to OMOP CDM.

✓ In Progress
Year 2 · Sep 2025 – Aug 2026

Biomarker Validation & PRECISE Training (Aim 1 & 2)

Finalize CT biomarker set with pathology ground truth validation; train initial PRECISE GNN with adversarial debiasing; ablation studies comparing unimodal and fusion models.

⬤ Current Year
Year 3 · Sep 2026 – Aug 2027

Model Refinement & Internal Validation

Hyperparameter tuning; fairness analysis by race/sex/site; comparison vs. ENDPAC and CAPS clinical scores; SHAP explainability integration; FHIR CDS Hooks API development.

Upcoming
Year 4 · Sep 2027 – Aug 2028

Prospective Deployment — Aim 3

Begin prospective validation at 4+ sites; integrate PRECISE into Epic EHR workflow; enroll first 500 prospective subjects; interim analysis at 12 months with DSMB review.

Upcoming
Year 5 · Sep 2028 – Aug 2029

Final Validation, Dissemination & Open-Source Release

Complete 24-month follow-up; final performance and equity analyses; manuscript submissions; open-source model release via GitHub; NCI progress report and renewal preparation.
